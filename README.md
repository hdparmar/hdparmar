### Hi, I'm Harshdeep

I'm an embedded engineer in Stockholm. I build devices that sense the world and make sense of it on their own: firmware, signal processing and small machine-learning models running at the edge, close to the sensor. Most days that means STM32 and ESP32, FreeRTOS, and DSP that has to fit in a few kilobytes, with embedded Linux when a device needs more room.

Right now I'm writing firmware for wireless lights that react to the room, making [Nadilo](https://tonestruments.se), a game that teaches beat-making by ear, and teaching myself embedded Linux from the ground up on a RISC-V Milk-V Duo.

Before that I worked on a Buildroot platform for spatial audio at ORB. During my master's at KTH I got neural networks running on microcontrollers, and for my thesis I fine-tuned Riffusion on Irish traditional music ([Tradi-fusion](https://github.com/hdparmar/Tradifusion)).

I also shoot film and write. Both live on [hdparmar.github.io](https://hdparmar.github.io).
